A working prototype of a location-aware event discovery app — spot what's happening around you, point your camera at something to find out what it is, plan a trip by month, and invite people along.

Real in this prototype
• GPS location from your device
• Live camera capture
• Distance calculated from where you actually are
• Maps and tiles (OpenStreetMap)
• Invites, saved events and itinerary — stored in this browser
• Shareable invite links
Simulated demo data
• Event listings — 44 seeded events across six cities
• Image recognition — returns a prepared result for your nearest city
• Translation — returns a prepared result
• "Get tickets" links are placeholders
To make it real
Swap the seeded data for live feeds from Ticketmaster, Eventbrite, Meetup and Yelp; route image recognition to a vision API; and add a backend for accounts and cross-device invites. The interface is built against a single internal data layer so that swap does not touch the screens.
